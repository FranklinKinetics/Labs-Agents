# Loop: kit setup (Truck Note to Invoice, $79)

- **Trigger:** a $79 sale in the Squarespace Labs store.
- **Owners:** Ines (form, booking, records), Mack (builds the kit; Sonnet, Haiku for files), Vera (checks before delivery).
- **Brandon's minutes:** 20 per sale, for the handover call. **Cap: 2 setups a week**; extra buyers get the next open slot, and the page never mentions limits.
- **Output:** a kit for that firm, delivered within 3 working days of a complete setup form, plus one handover call.

## Steps
1. **Sale.** Ines emails the setup form within a day (a Google Form owned by Brandon's Workspace, or a reply-by-email form until that exists).
2. **Form back.** Business details, rates, taxes, terms, logo, price list (a photo or file), number of trucks, and the AI they use. Stored in the private "Kit setups" Drive folder, **never this repo**.
3. **Build** (Mack): the firm's own ChatGPT and Claude links and Claude skill with their details and price book built in; truck cards with their QR code; the get-paid pack with their logo; the office routine. Test the longest link on a phone.
4. **Check** (Vera): their numbers match the form exactly, nothing is invented, the links open, and no other customer's data is in the files.
5. **Book** the handover call into Brandon's evening slots, and send the kit an hour before it.
6. **Handover call** (Brandon, 20 min): run one of their real work orders through it on screen. Last question: *"Where else does work wait in your shop?"* Ines logs the answer as a **contact** on the Signal ladder; any follow-up conversation is a **call**.
7. **Delete** the form and price list 30 days after delivery. Ines notes "deleted" with the date (no names) in the weekly review.

## Rules
- No promises about results on the call or in the kit.
- If a setup needs more than the 20-minute call (a custom integration, a second meeting), that's consulting: the call is where it's offered, unpriced.
- A refund request within 30 days is honoured without argument.
