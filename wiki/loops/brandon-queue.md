# Loop: Brandon's queue

- **Trigger:** anything that needs G1 Release, G2 Money, or a Vera flag.
- **Owner:** Ines. **Model:** Sonnet for packets; Haiku for housekeeping.
- **Brandon's minutes:** 45 a week at most. He clears it in his evening hour.

## Steps
1. Write the packet in the format in `canon/gates.md`: one screen, a verdict, ≤3 bullets, one decision.
2. Link the detail by path (clearance, draft, sources). Never paste it in.
3. Add the item to `QUEUE.md` under **Waiting on Brandon**, with an estimate of his minutes.
4. If the queue's total is over 45 minutes, Ines batches, defers or cuts until it fits, and notes what moved.
5. When Brandon answers (pass, pass with changes, or hold), move the item to **Done** with the date, log it in `logs/gates-log.md`, and act on it.
6. One-off setup tasks only Brandon can do (his accounts, his site) also go here, with step-by-step instructions linked.

## Rules
- Silence is not a pass. Unanswered items stay, and the weekly review notes their age.
- Never more than one decision per item.
