# Critique of plan v1 (one critic, Sonnet, 2026-11-26)

Brief: "Would this get a busy trades owner to call within 60 days, on 10 hours a week?"
Verbatim summary of the critic's report; the plan's response is in `labs-to-calls.md`, section 6.

**Verdict: Only if.** As written it probably produces replies from a few firms but not a booked call within 60 days, and several hours and gates are fictional.

1. **The hook is homework.** Pasting 60–90 days of job dates into an AI assumes an AI account, clean exports and quiet time. The owner reads email on a phone between sites, and the office manager knows the dates, not the owner. Fix: a one-minute, no-AI, no-data hook tied to the 28-day rule, plus a "reply with your number" ask; the AI check becomes an optional second step.
2. **The test can't tell success from noise; 30 and 60 days don't line up.** 40 emails at a few percent gives 1–3 replies; "1 call vs 0" is a coin flip. Fix: send all 40 in two weeks, read leading signals at day 10, judge at day 45, set the 60-day bar at a scoped engagement conversation, pre-agree the fail action (change the hook, not the channel). 2b: qualify headcount first or the denominator is junk.
3. **Hidden Brandon hours and single points of failure.** Reading drafts, packets, an unpaid 3-hour diagnostic, call prep, follow-up; evening-only slots; replies stall during flying stretches. Fix: a booking page with fixed evening slots, canned replies drafted by Haiku, a travel-week rule, cap the diagnostic.
4. **CASL and guardrail gaps.** Log the source URL and date for every address; no scraping against site terms (drop Procore); a suppression list and a working unsubscribe; Vera checks the business-owner paragraph for implied ROI; never put "$1k/month" on a page.
5. **The cuts remove safety nets where outreach is risky.** The planner also checks; Vera becomes the only reviewer; "unsure → don't ask Brandon" inverts the safe default for irreversible sends. Fix: outbound email as a G1 item per batch; escalate when unsure; an independent check on licences.

Missing: one place to log each prospect; first-touch attribution is unmeasurable for email; page views can't show which firm visited; ?ref is lost on a phone reply; no follow-up email; the trades firm Brandon already knows should be the first conversation.

**What it gets right (keep):** Labs judged by calls, not copies; one channel, one segment, one dated pain with a legal hook and citations; one CTA per page; a ladder that measures contacts and calls; test before building more.
