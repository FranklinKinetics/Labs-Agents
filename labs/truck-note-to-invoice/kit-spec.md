# Team Kit spec · CAD $49 (Ines, 2026-11-27)

Brandon approved $49 on the condition that the perceived value goes up: "right now it's just a PDF with instructions and links." The principle: **make it theirs, and put something physical in the truck.** Everything here runs on the buyer's own device; nothing is hosted and nothing comes back to us (decision 2026-11-10). Brandon's time needed: none (guardrail 8).

## What's in the kit (build order = value per crew hour)
| # | Piece | What the buyer gets | Effort | Why it raises the value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Kit Builder** (one HTML file, works offline) | Type in your business details, rates, taxes, terms, and **your price book** (your 20–50 most common parts and services) once. Out come **your own** one-tap ChatGPT and Claude links and **your own** Claude skill, with all of it built in. Techs never type a rate again. | Mack, ~1 session | It's their assistant, not ours. The price book is real value: line items priced from their own list, never guessed. |
| 2 | **Truck card** (printable, generated by the Kit Builder) | A card for the dashboard or the clipboard with a QR code for the firm's own link: *"Job done? Snap the work order."* One per truck. | Included in 1 | A physical object in every truck. The tool is visible to the whole crew, not hidden in the owner's phone. |
| 3 | **Get-paid pack** | A clean invoice layout (Word and Google Docs) with a logo slot; covering-email, day-20 reminder and day-35 firm-reminder wordings the AI fills in. | Fenn + Mack, ~½ session | It covers the whole stretch from job to paid, not one step. It also previews the automation (reminders that send themselves). |
| 4 | **Office routine card** | One page: the 2-minute daily "approve and send" routine for whoever runs the office. | Fenn, small | Turns a tool into a habit. It's the human version of the automation. |
| 5 | **Worked examples** | Three invented before/after pairs (a handwritten sheet, a voice note, a messy multi-day job), clearly labelled. | Mack, small | Shows the craft before they try it; also used on the page. |

**Delivered as:** a download, `Truck-Note-to-Invoice-Kit.zip`, containing `Start here.pdf` (a 2-page guide), `Kit Builder.html`, `Get-paid pack/` (docx, Google Docs link, the wordings), the routine card PDF, and the examples. The generic Claude skill is no longer in the kit: buyers make their own personalised one with the Builder.

## Rules (Vera)
- No claims about getting paid faster, saving hours, or anything else about results (guardrail 3). Describe what each piece does.
- The Kit Builder makes no network calls. It builds the links and the QR code in the browser, and the page says so.
- The price book stays on their device, inside their own links and skill.
- The reminder wordings are polite and factual: no threats and no legal language. "Firm" means clear, not threatening.
- Long personal links: a price book can make a link too long. The Builder must warn, and fall back to "copy and paste" when it does. Test the longest case on a phone.

## Not in the kit (on purpose)
- Anything that sends invoices or reminders by itself. That's the automation, and it's the Call Path ("If you want this happening on its own: book a call").
- A video from Brandon (his time). A Fenn script can come later if the page earns visits.
