## 1. The number and the package

**CAD $790, flat, for one company's team of 3 to 8 leaders.** Price shown before any applicable tax. Whether GST/HST applies depends on Franklin Kinetics' registration status, which Sol does not know. Brandon, check with the accountant before the page goes live.

**What is included**
- The setup kit (CEO guide, interviewer instructions for Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini, the private drop-box link, a leader invitation, the status link).
- One interview of about 20 minutes for each of 3 to 8 leaders, in the company's own AI.
- The leadership alignment report, as a PDF emailed to the CEO.
- One restart within 12 months of purchase if fewer than 3 leaders took part.
- One check-in email at 14 days.

**Flat, not per leader.** Reasons:
- One number is easier to say yes to. The CEO does not have to decide the headcount before buying.
- Per-leader pricing nudges the CEO to leave people out. That weakens the report.
- Our cost does not rise in any way that matters with 3 versus 8 blocks (see section 6).

**No credit toward the Beta.** A credit ("$X off the Beta") tells the reader the Beta has a price larger than $X. That brushes against guardrail 7. It also commits us to a number for a Beta whose range is untested. Keep the diagnostic standalone. If Brandon wants to credit the diagnostic later, do it privately in a proposal after a conversation, never on the page. The report's last section already carries the only offer.

**No founding or launch price.** One honest price from day one. If Brandon later wants an introductory price, it must state its real end date plainly, and the full price must show beside it.

**Checkout: Squarespace Commerce.**
- **Commerce is already on** for the Labs products (Brandon, 2026-11-10). No new plan needed, unless the current plan is Basic.
- **Fees, from https://www.squarespace.com/pricing, opened 2026-11-10** (shown in $, currency not labelled on the page as fetched):
  - Basic: 2% online store transaction fee, card rate from 2.9% + $0.30.
  - Core: 0% transaction fee, card rate from 2.9% + $0.30. Squarespace calls it "a foundation for service businesses".
  - Plus: 0%, card rate from 2.7% + $0.30. Advanced: 0%, from 2.5% + $0.30.
  - So on Core, a $790 sale costs about $23 in card fees and no transaction fee. On Basic, add about $16. Check which plan the site is on.
- **Needs to confirm:** custom checkout questions (province, platform, planned number of leaders), an unchecked optional consent box, two required consent boxes, and a stop for Quebec head offices. If Squarespace cannot do the Quebec stop in checkout, put a plain notice above the buy button and ask for province in the form. Mack confirms.
- The old "turn off commerce" housekeeping line was deleted from the website copy (master and repo) on 2026-11-10.
- **Digital product, no shipping.** Set it up as a service or digital product with no delivery address.

**Refund terms (plain words for the page)**

**For:** the CEO of an owner-led Canadian company of 20 to 200 people, outside Quebec, with 3 to 8 leaders. Someone who suspects their leaders pull in different directions and has no cheap, safe way to find out.

**The job:** "Show me where my leadership team agrees, where it differs, and what we still have to settle, without a consultant in the room and without my leaders feeling watched." The report shows gaps. It does not pick a main effort and does not promise results.

## 5. The Signal that would tell us the price is wrong

Starting thresholds, because traffic will be small and mostly arrives from How It Works. Revisit at the first Debrief.

- **Too high:** after 150 visits to the diagnostic page (from the Squarespace analytics) there are 0 or 1 purchases. Before touching the price, check whether visitors reach the buy button and whether the minimum-leaders line scares them off.
- **Too low:** the first 8 buyers all buy within a week of the page going live, none ask a question about the price, and none ask for the Beta. Then test $990 for the next 8.
- **Product, not price:** fewer than half of buyers get 3 or more leaders to take part within the window. That is a setup or invitation problem, not a price problem. Log it in `logs/friction-log.md`.
- **Wrong kind of buyer:** buyers who purchase but never invite leaders. Counts as a Signal on the page copy, not on the price.

Record the result as a Living Model in `models/price-model.md`. Never record a conversion rate that was not measured.
