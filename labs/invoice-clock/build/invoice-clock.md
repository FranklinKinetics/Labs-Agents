## How long do your invoices wait?
**Five questions. One minute. Count your "no" answers.**

1. **Does an invoice go out within 2 working days of a job being finished?**
2. **Is there one named person whose job it is to send it?**
3. **Can that person send it without waiting on paperwork from the truck** (a sign-off, timesheet or photos)?
4. **Could you say right now, without asking anyone, which invoices are past 28 days?**
5. **When an invoice is still unpaid at day 20, does a reminder go out without anyone remembering to send it?**

### Your score
- **0 "no"**: your clock is tight.
- **1–2 "no"**: one or two steps where an invoice can sit with no one moving it.
- **3–5 "no"**: invoices are waiting at several steps, and no one owns the wait.

Every "no" is a step where an invoice waits and no one is responsible for the wait. That's what a State Map finds.

### Why 28 days matters in Saskatchewan
For construction contracts made since 1 March 2022, Saskatchewan's prompt-payment rules give an owner 28 days to pay after receiving a *proper invoice*. The clock starts when a correct invoice arrives, not when the job ends. Every day before it's sent is a day the clock hasn't started. *(Sources: Government of Saskatchewan, 1 March 2022; Miller Thomson. This is general information, not legal advice.)*
