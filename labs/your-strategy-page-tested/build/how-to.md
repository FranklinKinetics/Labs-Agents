# Your Strategy Page, Tested
*Would it change a decision?*

## What it does
You paste one block of text into an AI chat, then paste your strategy page. The AI tests each line against five questions and tells you, in a short table, which lines would guide a decision and which would not. It usually takes a few minutes.

The five questions: Would the line change a decision? Does it name something good you give up? Could a new hire act on it on day one? Would it have pointed the right way on two or three real past decisions of yours? For a belief, is there a warning sign that would show it's wrong?

## How to use it
Built for any AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini). Tested so far in Claude. Same steps in each:
1. Open a **new chat**. (A fresh chat works best.)
2. Paste the whole text from `strategy-page-tested.txt` as your first message and send it.
3. When it asks, paste your strategy page as your next message. A one-pager, a values list, or slides copied as text all work.
4. When it asks for two or three real past decisions, type one sentence for each. You can skip this; those lines will be marked "needs your examples".

On a phone: open the file, select all, copy, then paste into the app's new chat.

## Privacy
This runs in your own AI, under your own account's terms. Franklin Kinetics never sees what you paste. Your AI provider handles what you paste under its own terms and settings. If your page has client names or other private details, swap in placeholders first.

## What it doesn't do
- It doesn't rewrite your strategy or suggest wording.
- It doesn't tell you what your strategy should be. You know your business better than any AI does.
- It doesn't judge whether your strategy is right, only whether the lines are clear enough to use.
- It doesn't promise any result. It is a read of the words on the page.
- AIs can make mistakes. Treat the verdicts as questions to think about, not rulings.
