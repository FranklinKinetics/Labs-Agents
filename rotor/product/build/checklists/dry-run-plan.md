## Stage 1 · Simulated company (now)
- **Setup:** a fictional company with four leaders, each played by an AI persona (kit in `../simulation/`).
- **What Brandon does:** runs waves 1–3 through the real interviewer and the A2–A7 helpers, does his real T4, T5, T6 and T9 work, and times every touchpoint. There is no Mirror: he writes the draft sheet himself. A8 is run against a few dilemmas.
- **Time:** about 6–8 h of Brandon over 2–3 weeks.
- **It tests:** every AI helper's "Never" rules (the planted traps), output formats, and Brandon's minutes against spec section 4.
- **It cannot test:** whether leaders feel heard, real politics, the Mirror, or the 4 pm calls.
- **Exit criteria:**
  - every planted trap is handled correctly after at most one fix;
  - A5 never ranks a candidate;
  - Brandon's minutes on T4–T6 and T9 are within 30% of budget, or the budgets are re-estimated before RP4 pricing work.
- **Labelling:** fictional. Never shown to anyone, never used as a result, case study or example (guardrails 4 and 5).

## Stage 2 · Founding intake (the first real clients)
- **Size:** two companies at most. They are told plainly that they are founding clients of a new program.
- **Measures:** the dry-run measures below (minutes, override rates, agent faults, leader experience, calendar fit) apply to them in full.
- **Brandon's protection:** a written, honest note that this is the program's first run. Brandon keeps extra margin: no Beta client in parallel with the founding intake's build.
- **Exit:** a one-page debrief by Ines feeds RP4 and the RP6 evidence.

## Original plan (kept for reference; its measures and exit criteria now apply to stage 2)


**Purpose:** run the whole program once, on a friendly company, before the first real intake. It tests the agents, the touchpoints and Brandon's real minutes. Nothing from the dry run is shown to anyone as a result or a case study (guardrails 4 and 5).

## Choosing the company
- **Owner-led, about 10–200 people, with 3–5 leaders** willing to give about 1.5 hours each over 6 weeks.
- **Someone who will give honest feedback** and doesn't need a polished experience.
- **Not a prospect Brandon hopes to sell the program to soon.** A dry run is not a free sale.
- **Brandon picks the company.** The crew does not suggest one.

## Agreement (one page, before starting)
- **It's a dry run:** the company gets a draft Rotor Sheet and Brandon's notes, with no guarantees.
- **The same data rules as the real program:** interviews in their AI, drafting in Brandon's own project for them, everything deleted at the end with written confirmation.
- **Nothing published:** no write-up, quotes or results without separate written permission later.

## What runs

The build is compressed to 6 weeks. The live run is shortened: 4 weeks instead of 12.

| Week | Step | Agents tested | Brandon touchpoints |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome pack and kickoff video; interviewer installed | A1 install check | T2, T3 (4 pm call) |
| 1–2 | Wave 1 | A1, A2 | T4, T5 |
| 2–3 | Wave 2 | A3, A1, A2, A4 | T4, T5 |
| 3–4 | Candidates and wave 3 | A5, A6, A1, A2 | T6, T4 |
| 5 | Pre-read and pre-Mirror video | A2 | T7 |
| 6 | **The Mirror**: remote, 2 h, on an evening or a planned day off | — | T8 |
| 6–7 | Sheet, script, red-team | A6, A7, A8 | T9 |
| 7–10 | Shortened live run: 2 flight logs, 1 pulse, noise audit before and after (4 weeks shows only whether the steps work, not any effect) | A9, A10, A11 | T10, T11, T12 |
| 11 | Results pack, results video and review call | A12 | T13 |

## What we measure
1. **Brandon's minutes per touchpoint**, logged the same day, against the budgets in spec section 4. This number matters most.
2. **The override log:** the change rate by step (S1–S11).
3. **Agent faults:** every time an agent breaks a "Never" rule or its output format. Each one becomes a fix before the first intake.
4. **Leader experience:** three questions at the end, by the A11 pulse format.
   - Did you feel heard?
   - Was there enough of Brandon?
   - What was annoying?
5. **Calendar fit:** did the 4 pm calls and the Mirror fit Brandon's week? What did it cost him?

## Exit criteria (all must hold before the first real intake)
- [ ] Brandon's total minutes are within 30% of the 1,292-minute budget, or the spec is re-estimated at RP4.
- [ ] No agent broke a "Never" rule on the second try, after its fix.
- [ ] A5 never ranked or recommended a candidate.
- [ ] The Mirror ended with a signed-off sheet.
- [ ] Data was deleted at close-out and confirmed in writing.

## After the dry run

- **Friction** goes in `logs/friction-log.md`.
