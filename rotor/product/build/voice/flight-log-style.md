# Flight log house style

The flight log is the CEO's short written update. It replaces a status meeting. It shows momentum, and it gives the CEO one useful thing to think about.

**When:** weekly through the build (weeks 1 to 9), every two weeks through the live run (weeks 9 to 20). Same day every time: [day].
**To:** the CEO only.
**Length:** under 300 words. Aim for 200.
**Who writes it:** A9 drafts it. You read every line and put it in your own words. Budget: 8 minutes.
**Optional:** a 3-to-5-minute phone video, only when there's something to show.

## The four parts, always in this order

1. **What we heard.** Themes, in the leaders' own recurring words. Never who said what.
2. **What's taking shape.** What's firming up, said provisionally: "so far", "it looks like". Before the Mirror, never present a main-effort candidate as the answer.
3. **What's next.** Dates and who does what. If the CEO has to act, say what and by when.
4. **One question.** Something only the CEO can answer, in a sentence or two. A real question, not a quiz or a nudge in disguise.

## Rules

- **Nothing traceable to one leader.** If only one person raised something, leave it out, or hold it for the divergence summary where it's handled with care. With five leaders, even "someone in operations" is a name.
- **Counts, not names, for progress.** "Four of five have finished wave 1." If someone is behind, you nudge them yourself. The CEO is not the enforcer.
- **Live run: team-level only.** Pulse findings are reported by team, never by person.
- **No early verdicts.** No "it's working", no percentages before the results pack, no money.
- **Their words, not ours.** Use the company's language from the harvest. Keep Rotor terms to the ones they've already met: main effort, guardrail, the Mirror, the Rotor Sheet.
- **Plain text.** Short sentences. No attachments needed to understand it.
- **Subject line:** `[company] · week [n] update`. (See the note below on the name.)
- **Sign off with your first name.** No "Best regards".

## Example (build, wave 1)

> **Subject:** [company] · week [n] update
>
> [CEO first name],
>
> **What we heard.** [Number] of [number] leaders have finished their first interview. [Theme one, using a phrase several leaders used, e.g. "(their recurring phrase)".] [Theme two, one sentence.] [Where views already line up, one sentence.]
>
> **What's taking shape.** It looks like [area] is where views differ most. Too early to say why. [Optional: one early image or story that keeps coming up.]
>
> **What's next.** Wave 2 questions go out [day]. Each one is built from that leader's own answers, so it should feel like a conversation picking up where it left off. [Anything you need to do, with a date, e.g. "Could you send (document) by (date)?"]
>
> **One question.** [A question only you can answer, e.g. "When (situation from the interviews) last came up, who made the call, and was it the right person?"]
>
> [Brandon]

## Live-run variant

Same four parts. "What we heard" draws on the pulse and the decision log, by team. "What's taking shape" is about how the guidance is behaving: where it helped, where it got in the way. "What's next" names the next pulse or audit date.

## Note on the name

"Flight log" is the internal name, from the playbook. The subject line above says "update" so client-facing copy doesn't lean on flying. Brandon decides.
