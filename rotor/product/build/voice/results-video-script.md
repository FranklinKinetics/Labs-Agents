# Results video script (T13, Rotor Distillation)

**Purpose:** show the CEO, and any leaders the CEO shares it with, the same evidence, honestly, before the review, so the review is spent deciding rather than presenting.
**Target length:** about 3 to 4 minutes (roughly 480 words spoken). Shorter is better. Cut any measure that wasn't agreed at kickoff.
**Adapt time:** about 15 minutes, after you've checked the results pack (A12). Every number comes from the pack. If it isn't in the pack, it isn't in the video.
**Sent:** at least [three] working days before the CEO review on [date].

**Fill before recording**
- [company] · [CEO first name] · [review date] · [beta team name]
- The measures agreed at kickoff, each with its result
- For the headline measure (how good the guidance was), pick the one block below that matches the result, and delete the other two.

**Hard rules for this video**
- Team-level results only. No individual is named or identifiable.
- No financial or ROI claims. Financial effects stay beliefs to test later.
- A null or mixed result is reported as plainly as a good one, in the same tone, at the same length.
- There is no comparison team. Never imply one. Say what one team over six weeks can't show.
- The decision is [CEO first name]'s, with you, at the review. Give your read as yours, and mark it.

---

[Direction: seated, plain. Charts on screen as simple bars, same scale throughout. No animation that flatters.]

Hi [CEO first name]. It's Brandon. This is what happened over the last six weeks, so you see the evidence before we meet on [review date] to decide what's next.

**How to read it** [about 30 seconds]

A reminder of the setup. The [beta team] worked with the Rotor Sheet in their AI tools for six weeks. Before they started, their lead wrote ten real dilemmas, and the team rated how the AI handled them. During the six weeks, they rated it again on real work, answered one short set of questions, and logged the moments the guidance changed a call. There's no comparison team, so I can't separate what the sheet did from a team simply paying more attention. These are early signs of how decisions are made. They are not financial results, and I won't pretend they are.

**The headline: was the guidance any good?** [about 1 minute]

[Direction: one chart. The team's ratings before go-live and in use.]

[IF CLEARLY GOOD] On the ten test dilemmas, the team rated [share] of the AI's answers as right, rather than too pushy, too quiet or wrong. In real use, [share]. That's one team over six weeks. It's an early, indicative signal, not proof.

[IF MIXED] Some of it worked and some didn't. On [type of decision], the team rated the guidance right [share] of the time. On [type of decision], [share]. My best guess at why is [reason]. That's a guess, and the review is where we test it.

[IF NOT GOOD] The honest answer is that the guidance didn't help much. The team rated [share] of its answers as right. That's a real result, and it's worth as much as a good one. The likeliest reasons I can see are [reason one] and [reason two]. I could be wrong about both.

**The supporting measures** [about 1 minute]

[Direction: one line each. Only the measures agreed at kickoff.]

- **Recall:** [share] of the beta team could name the main effort and two guardrails at week [n].
- **Decisions it changed:** [number] moments in the log where the guidance changed a call. [One example, by situation, not by person.]
- **Overrides:** [none worth noting / people kept overriding (guardrail), which suggests (it's too tight / it's unclear / it misses local knowledge)].
- **Tripwires:** [none fired / (number) fired: (which belief) and what it signalled].
- [For any measure that didn't move: "This one didn't show much: (result)." Say it in the same voice as the others.]

**What I'm not claiming** [about 15 seconds]

This doesn't tell us what any of it is worth in dollars. And one team for six weeks is one team for six weeks.

**My read** [about 30 seconds]

[Direction: say plainly that this is your view.]

My read, for what it's worth: [keep it as is / extend it to (which teams) / set up a quarterly review of the sheet / stop]. Because [one or two reasons, tied to the results above].

[Only if the overrides or reactions showed real disagreement about the main effort: "One more thing. Some of what came up looks less like a wording problem and more like a difference of view among your leaders. If that's right, the AI can't fix it, and the Rotor Beta is built for exactly that. Worth talking about."]

If you see it differently, you know [company] better than I do, and that's what the review is for.

**Close**

Please watch this before [review date], and send me any question you'd want answered there. And thank the [beta team] for six weeks of honest ratings. [Optional, only if true: one specific thing you saw the team do well.]

[Direction: cut. No music.]
