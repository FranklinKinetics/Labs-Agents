# Client tracker (Rotor Distillation)

**Brandon only.** Never share it with a client. It replaces the retired intake calendar (`intake-calendar.md`). Starts are rolling: each company starts when it signs and the fit check is done. Copy this file once, keep it in your private library, and use company codes, never names.

Budgets are Ines's estimates (rp2b-spec, section 2). They are living models: the first companies replace them with tracked minutes.

## The one rule

**At most two companies in weeks 1 to 4 at the same time.** (Week-2-to-3 sheet work is about 2 h per company, so two at once is the comfortable maximum.) Check this before you give any company a start date. If a third is ready, it waits.
