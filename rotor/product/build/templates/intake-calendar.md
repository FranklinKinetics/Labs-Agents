## The five calendar rules

Tick each when checked against this intake.

- [ ] 1. **Never two Mirror days within three weeks of each other**, including the beta's Mirror.
- [ ] 2. **Each intake's build overlaps no other build.** The beta starts after this intake's Mirror day, when this intake is in its light live run.
- [ ] 3. **The Mirror day is fixed at signing**, and every company in the intake signs knowing the date. Leave is requested once the first company signs.
- [ ] 4. **If only two companies sign, the intake runs with two.** If one, that company is offered the next intake or the beta. No intake runs with one company.
- [ ] 5. **No direct competitors in the same intake**, even though they never meet.
