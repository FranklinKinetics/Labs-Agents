# Brandon's AI account: training and retention check (Juno, 2026-11-10)

**Why this check:** the RP2 decision puts the program's AI drafting in Brandon's own account, with one private project per company and deletion at close-out. The rule is that the account tier's terms must exclude training on its content.

**How solid it is:** only one fact was verified on an official page: Claude Team needs at least 2 seats. Everything else comes from search snippets. The vendors' privacy and help sites were blocked from this session.

| Tier | Trains on content by default? | Meets the rule? | Minimum seats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Pro / Max (consumer) | Yes, unless switched off (snippet) | **No**: training is a setting, not a term | 1 |
| Claude Team / Enterprise | No, under the Commercial Terms (snippet) | **Yes** | **2 (verified)** |
| ChatGPT Plus | Yes, unless switched off (snippet) | **No** | 1 |
| ChatGPT Business / Enterprise | No (snippet) | **Yes** | not found |
| Gemini (consumer) | Yes, unless activity is off (secondary sources) | **No** | 1 |
| Gemini in Workspace | No, without the customer's permission (snippet) | **Yes** | not found |
| Microsoft 365 Copilot (business) | No (snippet) | **Yes** | conflicting sources |

**Retention after deletion:** unverified for every platform. One snippet says deleted ChatGPT data is removed within 30 days unless a legal hold applies.

## Recommendation
Use a **commercial tier**. **Claude Team** is the only route we could verify for one person, and it means paying for 2 seats.

Brandon checks these himself at sign-up, because they can't be verified from here:
- [ ] the current minimum seats;
- [ ] the training clause in the Commercial Terms;
- [ ] how long a deleted project persists on the vendor's side;
- [ ] whether the vendor can keep data under a legal hold;
- [ ] that deleting a project removes it from the vendor's side, not just from view.

**What the client is told:** state the vendor's real retention period in the governance one-pager, once confirmed. Don't promise faster deletion than the vendor gives.
