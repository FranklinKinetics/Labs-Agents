# TRMS Plan: This Year and Next
*Dale Whitfield, CEO. Draft for the leadership team, February.*

## 1. Where we are putting our main effort
"This is the one that changes what kind of company we are." This year our main effort is to win and deliver the grocery chain's regional refrigeration rollout, and to use it to open a presence in a third city. We have always won on being close to our customers. Now we want to be the firm a regional chain calls first.

"You don't get to the other side of the lake by looking at the water." We will move when the opening is there.

## 2. Service is the backbone
Roughly two-thirds of our revenue is service and maintenance agreements, and most of our customer loyalty comes from them. Service is the backbone. We will not compromise service quality to chase growth. **Our best technicians stay on planned maintenance and emergency calls**, because that is where customers know our name.

Our values: show up, tell the truth, finish what we start.

## 3. The rollout and the third city
The rollout is bigger than anything we have done. **The rollout gets first call on our best technicians and estimators**, so that we deliver it well. We expect a presence in a third city to open **within 18 months**, run first from the branch.

Targets for this year: sign the rollout contract; complete the first two rollout sites; raise on-time arrival for emergency calls; keep technician turnover below last year's.

## 4. What we believe
- Customers who see us on time on the small jobs give us the big ones.
- The new field-service system will be **fully adopted this year**, and dispatch and finance will stop using their spreadsheet.
- Technicians stay when they are paid fairly.
- A quote that arrives first usually wins.

## 5. Systems before sites
Service is the backbone, and the systems behind it have to hold up. **We will not open a new site until dispatch and finance run on one system, with no spreadsheet beside it.** Service is the backbone; we fix the core first.
