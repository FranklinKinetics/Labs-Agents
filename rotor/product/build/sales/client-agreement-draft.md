## 1. Parties

## 2. What we will do, and when

We will take the strategy you have already chosen and turn it into a **Rotor Sheet** that your team's AI tools can use. The work is the **Rotor Distillation**.

- **Length:** about 12 weeks from the start date of [DATE].
- **Three calls with your CEO:** a kickoff, a sign-off session and a final review. We offer 4 pm or evening times.
- **The fit check is done.** Before signing, we confirmed together that the leaders broadly agree on the strategy. If that turns out wrong during the work, we will say so and talk about next steps.
- **We draft; you decide.** Your CEO signs off the main effort and the Rotor Sheet. We never choose your strategy for you.
- **What is not included:** anything not described in this section, unless we agree it in writing.

## 3. What you provide

1. **Your strategy material** (plans, goals pages, offsite notes, values). You load it into your own AI using our document helper (A13) and email us the extraction it produces.
2. **A beta team of five or more people** who already use AI tools at work, named at kickoff.
3. **IT time** to load the Rotor Sheet and the Rotor Core into your team's AI tools, following our guide.
4. **Reasonable availability** from your CEO for the three calls and a short interview, and from other leaders where agreed.
5. **A list of exclusions:** topics that never go into any interview, AI project or document (for example acquisitions, pending personnel decisions, legal matters, unannounced financials).

## 5. How quickly we reply

## 6. Confidentiality, both ways

Each side keeps the other's confidential information private, uses it only for this work, and shares it only with people who need it to do the work.

- **Yours:** your strategy, your people, your numbers, and anything you mark confidential.
- **Ours:** our methods, templates and the Rotor Core (see section 8).

This does not cover information that is public, that the other side already had, or that the law requires us to share. If the law requires it, we tell the other side first where we can. This lasts [X years] after the work ends.

## 7. Your data

This is how your information is handled.

1. **Interviews and the document helper run in your own AI account.** The full conversations and your strategy documents stay there.
2. **What we receive, by email:** each leader's summary block, and the CEO's extraction from the document helper (A13). **We never receive your documents or any full transcript.**
3. **Where we draft.** In our own AI account. One private project for you, used for no other client. Training on that account's content is switched off in its settings, and we confirm that in writing before the first block arrives.
4. **Deletion.** When the work ends, we delete your project and every copy we hold, including the emails. We send you written confirmation within [X working days] of close-out.
5. **The vendor's retention.** The AI vendor may keep deleted content for a set time. For this account that is [X days]. We confirm it with you before kickoff.
6. **Quotes are unattributed** unless that leader agrees otherwise.
7. **No report on a group smaller than five.** Results are reported by team, never about a person.
8. **Our own notes** about how our methods perform contain no names, no quotes and no strategy content.

## 8. Who owns what

**You own:**
- your Rotor Sheet, and
- your data.

**Franklin Kinetics owns:**
- our methods and templates, and
- the Rotor Core.

**Licence to the Rotor Core.**
- We license the Core to you for **internal use by your beta team, during this engagement.**
- The licence **ends at close-out** unless we agree in writing to renew it.
- You will not share it outside your company, resell it, or use it to build a competing product.
- Your copy carries a copyright notice. Copies may be traceable.
- At close-out, you remove the Core from your AI tools [and confirm in writing].

Your Rotor Sheet stays yours after the licence ends.

## 9. No guarantee of results

We do our work with care. We do not promise any business result, and we make no promise about money earned or saved. We measure how well people know the strategy and how the guidance rates, and we report the results honestly, including where nothing changed.

## 10. How the AI behaves

The AI in your team's project assumes your people know more about their situation than it does. It asks questions, raises issues in proportion to the stakes, and says so plainly if a choice crosses a guardrail you agreed. **It never decides for anyone.** A person can always override it. It is not used in performance reviews.

## 11. Being open with your employees

Before the beta team starts, they receive a one-page note on what the AI carries and what it logs, and a plain-language summary of how it behaves. Everyone in the company can read the Rotor Sheet. Leadership and compliance staff may read the Rotor Core under confidentiality. You are responsible for any notice your local rules require.
