**Subject:** Welcome to the Rotor Distillation: what happens next

Hi [CEO first name],

Thank you for choosing to work with me. Here is what the next few weeks look like, and the one thing I need from you first.

**Two dates to hold now, and one to follow**
- **Kickoff, one-on-one with you:** [KICKOFF DATE], [4:00 pm / evening time] [time zone]. 45 minutes, on [PLATFORM]. We settle scope, what stays out, which team goes first and how we will measure it.
- **Sign-off session, one-on-one with you:** [SIGN-OFF DATE], [4:00 pm / evening time] [time zone]. 60 minutes. You settle the open items and sign off the main effort and the Rotor Sheet.
- **Review one-on-one with you:** about twelve weeks from now. I will confirm the day at the sign-off session.

If an evening suits you better than 4:00 pm, say so. I offer evenings first.

**What I need from you this week**
1. **Paste your existing strategy material into a helper in your own AI.** That means plans, EOS or OKR pages, offsite notes, values, and sayings people repeat. Rough is fine. The helper reads it and produces a short extraction block: what the documents say, in their own words, plus anything they leave open or say two ways. Read the block, correct it, and email it to me. **Do not send me the documents.** I will send you the helper and a two-line how-to. Anything confidential or off-limits stays out: remove it before you paste.
2. Ask whoever looks after your IT to go through the attached checklist. It is short.
3. Read the one-page governance note and the disclosure note. They say who sees what, where your information is kept and how it is deleted. Tell me at kickoff if anything should change.
4. Think about which team will use the Rotor Sheet first. It needs five or more people, who already use AI tools at work.

**What happens next**
After the kickoff you have one 20-minute conversation with an AI interviewer, on [PLATFORM], whenever suits you. One or two of your leaders may do the same if you wish. At the end, the interviewer reads back a short summary. You correct it and email it to me. The full conversation stays in your own account, and I do not receive it.

I then draft your Rotor Sheet from your own strategy: sharper wording of the main effort you have already chosen, guardrails, the beliefs it rests on and the signs that would tell you a belief is failing. I do not choose your strategy. I work on the draft myself in my own AI account, in a private project for [COMPANY] only, with training switched off in its settings. When I have judged and rewritten the draft, your leaders can send short written reactions, and we meet for the sign-off.

Then your IT contact loads the Sheet into your AI tools, using a guide I send, and your team uses it for six weeks. I send a short update every two weeks.

**How I work with you.** I work on this around other commitments, so I keep to a reply standard: every message you send me gets a reply within three working days. If something is urgent, put "urgent" in the subject line and I will answer that first.

If any of this is unclear, reply to this email.
