# Sign-off session agenda: CEO one-on-one (Rotor Distillation)

For Brandon. 60 minutes, live, one-on-one with [CEO name] at [COMPANY]. **4:00 pm or in the evening.** Date: [SIGN-OFF DATE]. On [PLATFORM]. Prep: about 20 minutes. Send the draft Rotor Sheet two days earlier, with the leaders' reactions (unattributed, de-identified) and a one-page list of open items.

**Purpose.** The CEO settles the open items and signs off two things: the main effort, in the wording the company chooses, and the Rotor Sheet. The strategy is the company's. Your job is to show the draft, show what is still open, and write down what the CEO decides.

**Prep (20 min).** Read the leaders' reactions (summary blocks, 20 minutes, separate). List the open items: each a gap or contradiction the draft flagged, or a point where reactions differ. Do not recommend a resolution beforehand. Bring A6's five-tests mark-up and be ready to say which lines pass or fail, and which fail only because something is not yet decided.

| Min | Item | What gets decided |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | What this session is for | Nothing; set the tone. The CEO has the draft and the open-item list. |
| 5–20 | **The main effort** | The CEO reads the two or three phrasings of the main effort they already chose, and the original wording. The CEO picks one, mixes them or keeps their own words. You do not recommend. |
| 20–40 | **Open items** | Each gap or contradiction in turn. For each: the CEO's decision, or "leave open, with an owner and a date". Guardrails and living models the CEO wants cut, changed or added. |
| 40–45 | **Hindsight check** | Test 2–3 lines against real past decisions. The CEO names the decisions; note which lines would have pointed the right way. |
| 45–50 | **The language map and tripwires** | Which of the company's own words stay. Who owns each tripwire. Which models are load-bearing. |
| 50–57 | **Sign-off** | The CEO states the main effort and approves the Sheet, or names what must change first and when it comes back. Record the exact wording and the date. |
| 57–60 | **Next steps** | IT loads the Sheet and the Core using the loading guide. The employee note and behaviour summary are published. The team lead writes ten dilemmas for week 5. |

**If sign-off is not given.** Do not load the Sheet. Agree what must change, who does it and the new date. If the open items turn out to be disagreements between leaders rather than gaps, say so plainly and talk through moving to the Beta (the CEO's call).

**After the call.** Send the signed-off main effort and the final Sheet in writing within three working days. Log your minutes in `../templates/client-tracker.md` and S5 and S6 changes in the override log.
