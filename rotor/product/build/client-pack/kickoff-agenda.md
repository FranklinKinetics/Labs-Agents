# Kickoff agenda: CEO one-on-one (Rotor Distillation)

For Brandon. 45 minutes, live, one-on-one with [CEO name] at [COMPANY]. **4:00 pm or in the evening** (offer the evening first). Date: [KICKOFF DATE]. On [PLATFORM]. Prep: about 15 minutes beforehand: read the A13 extraction (20 minutes, separately, week 0). Send this agenda two days earlier.

**Purpose.** Agree scope, what stays out, which team goes first and how it will be measured, how we will work together, and the sign-off date. Nothing here is a surprise: the welcome email named most of it.

**Opening line.** "In about twelve weeks, we want a Rotor Sheet that says your strategy the way you meant it, loaded into your team's AI tools, used for six weeks, with an honest read on whether it helped."

**Before the call, from the A13 extraction.** Pick the two or three gaps or contradictions it flagged that matter most. You will not settle them here; you will ask the CEO to think about them before the interview. If the CEO did not send an extraction, the CEO reads it aloud, or summarises it, in the Scope slot, and you take notes.

| Min | Item | What gets decided |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Welcome and what this meeting is for | Nothing; set the tone. Confirm 45 minutes is right. |
| 3–10 | **Scope** | Which team, business unit and planning horizon the Rotor Sheet covers. Who approves changes (one named executive). Which AI tools the team uses. |
| 10–18 | **Their documents** | Read back the extraction in outline. Which document is current. Name the gaps and contradictions you will ask about in the interview. No answers needed today. |
| 18–24 | **Exclusions** | The list of what never goes into any interview, any AI project or any document: acquisitions, pending personnel decisions, legal matters, unannounced financials, and anything else the CEO names. Write it down. |
| 24–31 | **The first team** | Who is on the beta team. Confirm it is five or more people, and that they already use AI tools at work. Who the team lead is (they write ten dilemmas in week 5). |
| 31–37 | **Measures** | Agree what we track: guidance quality (red-team ratings plus one in-use rating), recall of the main effort and two guardrails (one pulse), a decision log of moments the guidance changed a call, overrides and tripwires. Agree when a baseline is taken. No financial measures. |
| 37–42 | **How we work** | The reply standard: three working days, urgent items flagged and answered first. The CEO's day-to-day contact. How leaders email me their summary block. Who at IT loads the Sheet, and when. Confirm the governance one-pager and disclosure note. |
| 42–45 | **Close** | Read back what was agreed. Confirm the sign-off date and time slot. Ask what is missing. |
