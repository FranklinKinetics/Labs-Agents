# Kickoff agenda: CEO one-on-one

For Brandon. 45 minutes, live, 4:00 pm, one-on-one with [CEO name] at [COMPANY]. Date: [KICKOFF DATE]. On [PLATFORM]. Prep: about 15 minutes beforehand. Send this agenda two days earlier.

**Purpose.** Agree scope, how we will measure it, what stays out, who is in the beta and comparison teams, how we will work together, and the Mirror date. Nothing here is a surprise: the welcome email already named most of it.

**Opening line.** "By the end of the first quarter, we want a distilled strategy your leaders agree on, in use by one team, and honest evidence of whether it changed how they work."

| Min | Item | What gets decided |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Welcome and what this meeting is for | Nothing; set the tone. Confirm 45 minutes is right. |
| 3–10 | **Scope** | Which business unit and planning horizon the Rotor Sheet covers. Who approves changes (one named executive). |
| 10–20 | **Measures** | Agree two or three measures beyond the noise audit. Options: leader agreement, recall of the main effort, where time goes (a one-week sample), decision speed on two or three recurring decisions. Agree when baselines are taken. |
| 20–25 | **Exclusions** | The list of what never goes in: acquisitions, pending personnel decisions, legal matters, unannounced financials, and anything else the CEO names. Write it down. |
| 25–32 | **Beta team and comparison team** | Which team takes part and which acts as the comparison. Check they are similar in size and kind of work. The comparison team will not receive the strategy. Confirm the team is at least five people. |
| 32–37 | **How we work** | The reply standard: three working days, urgent items flagged and answered first. Who is the CEO's day-to-day contact. How leaders send their summaries to Brandon. Confirm the governance one-pager and the disclosure note. |
| 37–42 | **The Mirror** | Confirm [MIRROR DAY DATE] and the time slot. Remote or in person at the CEO's office. Who must attend. Confirm the fall-back: if the company is not ready by week 6, it moves to the next intake's Mirror day. |
| 42–45 | **Close** | Read back what was agreed. Ask what is missing. Confirm the next step and the review window. |
