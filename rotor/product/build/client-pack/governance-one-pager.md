## Interview data and where it goes

- **Who reads what.** Brandon reads the full interview transcripts. No one else at [COMPANY] does. What reaches the leadership team is a summary of where views agree and differ. Quotes are unattributed unless the leader agrees otherwise.
- **Where interviews run.** In [COMPANY]'s own [PLATFORM] account, under your existing agreement with that vendor.
- **Where summaries go.** Each leader emails Brandon their interview summary block and a copy of the conversation (the chat export), as the interviewer asks at the end of each session. Only Brandon reads them. The drafting of syntheses, summaries and progress notes runs in Brandon's own AI account. It is one private project for [COMPANY], named by a code, and used for no other client.
- **Training.** Training on this account's content is switched off in its settings. Brandon confirms this in writing before the first interview. [CONFIRMATION DATE]
- **What stays out.** No one in [COMPANY] needs to create an account for Brandon.
- **Close-out.** At the end of the engagement, Brandon deletes the project and every copy he holds, and sends [COMPANY] a written confirmation of the deletion by [DATE, within X working days of close-out]. That includes the summary-block emails in his inbox. The vendor's own retention for deleted projects is [X days, confirmed before kickoff].
- **Brandon's own notes.** Brandon keeps method notes about how his own tools perform. They contain no names, no quotes and no strategy content.

## How the AI behaves

The AI in your beta team's project keeps quiet unless something matters. It answers as normal most of the time. When a choice touches the strategy, it asks one light question. When a choice clearly crosses an agreed guardrail, it says so plainly and hands the decision back to the person. It assumes people know more about their situation than it does. A person can always override it, and the AI accepts that.

## What this program does not do

- It does not report on any individual. Pulse answers are read only by Brandon and reported by team, and no report covers a group smaller than five people.
- It is not used in performance reviews.
- It makes no promise about financial results. It measures how consistently people decide and how well they know the strategy, and reports the results honestly, including where nothing changed.
