# Fit-check wave (Rotor Distillation)

For Brandon. This is the wave file for the **fit check**: the short conversation that tells you whether the leaders broadly agree on where the company puts its main effort (Distillation) or clearly do not (Beta, or the diagnostic first). The Interviewer Pack stays unchanged: same core instructions, same summary block, same disclosure note (`../client-pack/disclosure-note.md`). Only the wave file changes.

**Who and when.** The CEO and one or two other leaders, each in one session of about 15 to 20 minutes, in the client's own AI, after discovery and after the disclosure note is sent. Skip it for a company whose leaders share their diagnostic report. Brandon receives the summary blocks by email, nothing else.

**What it is for.** The summary block's "Main effort (their words)", "Trade-offs they named" and "How they would resolve a collision" fields let you put the leaders' main efforts side by side (A2 can combine the blocks). It is not an assessment of the company. You decide, never AI, and never automatically. If the main efforts clearly differ, recommend the Beta or the diagnostic first (rp2b-spec, section 7).

**What it leaves out on purpose.** Beliefs, friction, images and outside-work questions. Fields for questions not asked come out as "none" in the summary block; that is expected.

The file to attach is the block below. Copy it and delete this header.

```markdown
# Fit-check questions
This is a short session of about 15 to 20 minutes. Open by saying it is a quick check on how the person sees the company's main effort, that there are no right answers, and that they can skip anything. Do not mention or compare with any other leader. Ask in this order. ESSENTIAL questions first if time is short. Do not ask anything not listed here.

1. ESSENTIAL: In one sentence, where is this company putting its main effort this year, and why?
2. ESSENTIAL: What are we deliberately giving up or saying no to, to make that happen?
3. ESSENTIAL: When two good things collide, such as a big new client versus serving existing ones, which way should people lean? Please give a real example from the past year if you can.
4. When your people explain the main effort to a new hire or a client, what words do they usually use?
```

## Check before the first use

- [ ] The session stays within 20 minutes and asks only these five questions.
- [ ] The interviewer never says what any other leader answered, and never says whether the person's answer "fits".
- [ ] The summary block comes out in the exact standard format, with "none" in every field the questions did not cover (for example beliefs, friction).
- [ ] A vague answer to Question 1 gets at most two follow-ups for a real example, then moves on.
- [ ] Question 4 records that material exists and what kind; no contents are asked for or recorded.
