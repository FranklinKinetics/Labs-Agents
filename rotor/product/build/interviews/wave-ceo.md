# CEO interview wave (Rotor Distillation)

For Brandon. This is the wave file for the **CEO interview**, week 1 to 2 of the Distillation. It is wave 1's essentials, with a short block of open points from the CEO's own strategy documents (from A13's extraction). The Interviewer Pack stays unchanged. Run it in the client's own AI. The CEO emails you the summary block only.

**How you fill it in.** Before you attach the file, read the A13 extraction and write up to three open points in the "Open points" section below: each a neutral question about a **gap** or **contradiction** the extraction flagged, quoting the documents' own words. Do not suggest an answer, and do not fill the gap yourself. If there are no open points, delete the section. One to two more leaders may do the same session on this file, with their own "Open points" deleted: the leader names in the file decide who gets them.

**Length.** About 20 minutes. ESSENTIAL questions first if time is short.

The file to attach is the block below. Copy it, fill in the open points and delete this header.

```markdown
# CEO interview questions
This is a session of about 20 minutes. Open by saying it builds on the company's existing strategy material and that the person knows far more about it than you do. Do not summarise or read the material back. Ask in this order. ESSENTIAL questions first if time is short.

## Open points from the company's own documents (CEO only)
Ask these after Question 3, one at a time, in the CEO's own terms. Do not suggest an answer. If the person says it is settled or not important, record that and move on.
- <Open point 1: "<quote A>" and "<quote B>" appear in different documents. How do they fit together?>
- <Open point 2: the documents do not say <gap>. What is the answer today?>
- <Open point 3, optional>

## Questions
1. ESSENTIAL: In one sentence, where is this company putting its main effort this year, and why?
2. ESSENTIAL: What are we deliberately giving up or saying no to, to make that happen?
3. ESSENTIAL: When two good things collide, such as a big new client versus serving existing ones, which way should people lean?
4. What do we believe about our market, customers or people that, if wrong, would change the plan?
5. What would tell you early that one of those beliefs is failing?
6. ESSENTIAL: What slows this company down most? Where does work wait?
7. What did we decide in the past year that didn't stick? Why not?
8. When this company is at its best, what is it like? What image or story captures it?
9. Which team would you pick to use AI tools with the strategy loaded first, and which decisions do they make most often?
```

*Question 9 replaces wave 1's money-and-people and outside-work questions. Its answer feeds the kickoff's scope and the team lead's dilemmas. If a platform has trouble placing it in the summary block, it goes under "Questions they want the leadership team to settle".*
