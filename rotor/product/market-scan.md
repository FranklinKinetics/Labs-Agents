# Rotor Product · market scan (Juno, 2026-11-10)

Input to the RP1 options memo. Written from search summaries only; Juno did not open the pages. Prices are quoted with their source for Sol's internal use. Guardrail 7 still applies: no consulting or Beta price appears anywhere public. "Unverified" means the source was weak or undated.

## 1. Productized strategy offers
- **EOS One** (software) is self-serve. The first user is free; extra users are USD 10/user/month ([eosone.com](https://eosone.com/?p=237)). Coach time is sold separately. EOS publishes no price sheet ([Demand Gen Report](https://legacy.demandgenreport.com/the-true-cost-of-eos-decoding-current-eos-membership-prices-and-value)).
- **Scaling Up** runs a clear ladder:
  - Book: USD 20.
  - Online course plus 8 coaching hours for an executive team of 8 (no price shown).
  - Private 1–3 day workshops: USD 6,000–16,000.
  - Ongoing coaching: USD 60,000–180,000 a year.
  - Sources: [Scaling Up](https://scalingup.com/scaling-up-pricing-packages-rockefeller-habits) and a [coach page](https://coaches.scalingup.com/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-implement-scaling-up-in-your-business). Both are undated.
- **Pattern:** a cheap self-serve entry, and live expert time sold in bounded, named blocks.

## 2. Cohort and group models
- **Vistage:** 12–16 CEOs, one full-day meeting a month, plus a 1–2 hour 1:1 with the chair each month ([Vistage](https://vistage.com/about-vistage/what-is-vistage/), [LongAngle](https://www.longangle.com/blog/is-vistage-worth-it)). A third party reports about USD 1,300–1,800 a month (unverified).
- **EO forums:** 6–10 entrepreneurs, meeting monthly, peer-led ([EO](https://eonetwork.org/one/executive-peer-groups/)).
- **Group coaching programs:** typically 6–15 people, with weekly 60–90 minute calls over 4–12 weeks; 8 weeks is most common ([Ruzuku](https://www.ruzuku.com/learn/articles/coaching-program-template), [Kajabi](https://www.kajabi.com/blog/group-coaching-math)).
- **Gap:** no evidence found on cohorts made up of companies rather than individuals.

## 3. Async-first consulting
- One example only: a PDF audit plus a recorded walkthrough of about 15 minutes, delivered in 48 hours ([Septim Labs](https://septimlabs-hq.pages.dev/audit/), unverified).
- No evidence found on how buyers value async access to an expert. Treat this as unknown.

## 4. AI alignment and strategy tools
- **OKR platforms** (e.g. Quantive) automate tracking and summaries. People still set the strategy.
- **AI interviewers** exist (Perspective AI, Outset, Conveo), aimed at employee feedback and market research. None found is aimed at leadership alignment.
- **Pattern:** tools automate collecting and summarizing, and leave interpretation and the decision to people. No tool found does the whole alignment job.

## Implications (Juno)
- A cheap self-serve entry plus bounded live blocks is the common shape. Rotor's three live sessions already fit it.
- Group formats cluster around one live meeting a month. Weekly group calls would break the 5-hour budget once prep is counted.
- Buyers pay most for live access at the top end. Async-only access is untested, so test it with the first clients.
- AI-led leader interviews plus a human Mirror looks like an open gap.
