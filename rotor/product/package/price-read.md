# RP4 price read (Sol, 2026-11-11)

Track recommendation for the two Lab parts. Each is confirmed or changed at its own G5, after its own Brief. Consulting parts (the intake program, the Beta) are not priced here and nothing below implies a price for them. No sales or conversion data exists; none is claimed.

## 1. Alignment diagnostic

**Free layer.** Each leader runs the AI interview in their own AI and gets their own short summary back. The interview alone is useful to a leader thinking about where they stand, and needs nothing from us.

**Paid layer.** The combine step: the agent instructions that merge three or more leaders' summaries into a divergence report (where the team agrees, where it differs, which trade-offs are unsettled), plus a one-page guide to reading it. Same rules as the spec: no report for fewer than three leaders, quotes unattributed, runs in the client's own AI, nothing hosted.

**Price and format.** Recommend **CAD 199, one-off, per company** (one run for up to about eight leaders). Range to test: CAD 149 to 299. Per company, not per leader, because the job is a team's picture and a per-leader price punishes a bigger team for doing the right thing. Not a subscription.

**Checkout.**
- Platform still TBD, to be settled at the first G5.
- Show the price in CAD, tax-inclusive or clearly "plus GST" (accountant to confirm).
- Collect only an email and payment; no account.
- One buyer pays for the company.
- The price is stated once, with no countdown, no pre-ticked extras, no "limited seats".
- Flag to Brandon as revenue nears the GST/HST registration threshold.

**Refund, plain words.** "If it didn't work for you, tell us within 30 days and we refund the full price. No form, no reasons required." (Brandon to confirm the 30 days.)

**Comparables** (all from search summaries only; Sol did not open the pages; prices likely USD and may have changed):
1. Custom Insight Team Assessment: about $95 per team plus a $0 to $750 setup fee. [source](https://custominsight.net/team-assessment/pricing.asp) (unverified)
2. Team Performance Inventory: about $600 per team, with survey administration, analysis and a summary report. [source](https://pda.us.com/?p=430) (unverified)
3. The Five Dysfunctions of a Team Assessment: $56.50 per person. [source](https://tablegroup.com/?p=3925) (unverified). Also noted: Working Genius at about $25 per person retail, no team grid or debrief ([comparison](https://www.consultclarity.org/post/working-genius-vs-other-assessments-full-price-comparison), unverified).

Read: team-level tools sit from about $95 to $600, and none found does an AI-run leader interview. CAD 199 is above the cheapest and well below the report-with-analysis end. It does not read as cheap (PM2).

**Who and what job.** An owner or leader of a firm with a leadership team of three or more who suspects the team sees the business differently. The job: show, in one sitting, where they agree and where they don't. It describes the gaps and does not promise any result (guardrail 3).

**Also the waiting room and wave 0.** It does both jobs as a side effect of being a good product. Its price is its own: a one-off for the report, stated nowhere as a deposit, credit or first instalment toward anything else.

**Signal the price is wrong.** After 50 visits to the product page, conversion under 1%, or Debriefs where buyers say the report was worth "much less" or "much more" than the price. Either way, revisit before the next 50 visits. (The thresholds are a starting guess, not data.)

## 3. Guardrail 7 check: Lab page wording

| Risk | Why | Safe alternative |
|---|---|---|
| "Upgrade to the program" / "next tier" | Implies a priced rung above | "Also from Franklin Kinetics: help settling what the report shows. Start a conversation." |
| "Credit toward", "deposit", "your CAD 199 counts" | States a consulting price by subtraction | Say nothing about money beyond this product |
| "Affordable first step", "entry price" | Implies the full path costs more | "A one-off report for your team" |
| "Graduate", "Beta price", "founding rate" | Implies a price list behind the page | "Companies that want to go further can start a conversation" |
| "Save time on the program" | Implies a priced program | Describe only what the report shows |
| "Limited intake spots" | Fake scarcity (guardrail 8) | State real next start dates only if true, with no urgency framing |

Also keep the buyer's follow-up offer a single plain link. Guardrail 8 on Brandon's time: the Lab never promises a call, review or reply from him.
