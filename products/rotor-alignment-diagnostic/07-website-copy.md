### Why it matters

Headline: **Most leadership teams think they agree. Few have checked.**

Every leader on your team could tell you the company's main effort. The trouble is they might each tell you something different. You won't hear it in meetings, because meetings reward agreement. You'll see it later, in decisions that pull in different directions.

The diagnostic asks each leader the same questions, separately, before anyone hears anyone else's view. Then it puts the answers side by side.

### How it works

1. **You buy it and get a setup kit by email.** It works with Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot or Google Gemini.
2. **You add the AI interviewer to your company's AI platform.** One paste. About 15 minutes. [CONFIRM time at dry run]
3. **Your leaders take the interview, on their own time.** Three to eight leaders, including you. About 20 minutes each, by voice or text.
4. **You get the report by email.** You close the interviews once at least three leaders are in, or they close on their own after three weeks.

### What you get

A written report on your leadership team, built from your leaders' own words:

- Where your team agrees
- Where it differs, starting with each leader's own answer to "what's our main effort?", side by side
- The trade-offs that aren't settled
- What those differences may lead to
- The beliefs your plan rests on, and the warning signs your leaders named
- Where work waits
- Your team's own images and sayings
- The questions your team needs to settle

The report shows the gaps. It doesn't tell you which view is right. That's for your team to settle.

Headline: **Your leaders can be candid, because no one is watching.**

- **No report for fewer than three leaders.** With only two, anyone could work out who said what.

### Questions

**Which AI platforms does it work with?**
Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and Google Gemini. The setup kit has steps for each.

**Do I need my IT team?**
Only if you need permission to add an assistant to your company's AI platform. Most CEOs can do it themselves in about 15 minutes.

**Can I see what each leader said?**
No. That's the point. Leaders tell an AI what they might not say in a meeting. The report shows the whole team's views without saying who said what.

**What if a leader doesn't want to take part?**
That's their choice. The report needs at least three. Leaders can skip any question, stop at any time, or leave something out of their summary.

**What happens to the data?**
The interview stays in your company's AI account. Each leader's summary comes to a sealed drop-box, where names and roles are removed on arrival. The summaries are deleted once your report is delivered. If you agree at checkout, I keep your report for my own research, never shared and never named. [CONFIRM wording with the privacy notice]

**Will it tell us what to do?**
No. It shows where your views line up and where they don't, and the questions you need to settle. Settling them is your team's job.

**What comes after?**
That's up to you. Many teams settle the questions themselves. If you'd like help, that's what the Rotor Beta does.
