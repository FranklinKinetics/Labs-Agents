# How the Rotor Alignment Diagnostic handles information

*Last updated: [date of publishing]*

Franklin Kinetics Ltd. [CONFIRM legal name] runs the Rotor Alignment Diagnostic. This notice explains what information the diagnostic uses, why, who can see it, and when it is deleted. It is written for two groups: the CEO who buys the diagnostic, and the leaders who take the interview.

## The short version

- **No person reads the interviews.** Not your CEO, and not Franklin Kinetics.
- **The interview stays in your company's own AI account.** We never receive it.
- **Only your summary comes to us**, and only if you choose to send it.
- **Your name and role are removed** the moment it arrives.
- **Summaries are deleted** once your company's report is delivered.
- **No report is made from fewer than three leaders.** Quotes are never attributed.

## What we collect, and why

| Information | From | Why | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer's name, email, company name, province, AI platform, planned number of leaders, consent choices | The CEO, at checkout | To deliver the diagnostic, send the report, and keep business records | As long as tax and business record rules require |
| Each leader's interview summary | The leader, only if they send it | To write the report | Until the report is delivered, then deleted |
| A one-way code made from each leader's name | Created from the summary on arrival; the name itself is not kept | To catch the same summary sent twice | Deleted with the summaries |
| The report | Written from the summaries | To deliver it to the CEO; kept afterwards only if the CEO agrees at checkout | Deleted at delivery, unless the CEO agreed we may keep it |
| A deletion record (date and number of summaries) | Created by our system | To show that deletion happened | Kept; it holds no content |

**What we never collect:** the interview conversation, leaders' email addresses, or anything leaders choose to leave out. We don't email leaders, and we don't track them.

## Names and roles

Each summary arrives with the leader's name and role, because the interview's summary format includes them. Our system removes both before anything is stored, wherever they appear in the summary. If a leader names a colleague, the interviewer writes "a colleague" instead. A separate automatic check reads every report for names, roles or identifying details before it is sent. If it finds any, the report is held until it is fixed.

## Who sees what

| | The interview | A leader's summary | The report |
|---|---|---|---|
| The leader | Yes | Yes. They check it and decide whether to send it | Only if the CEO shares it |
| The CEO | No | No | Yes |
| Franklin Kinetics | No | No person reads it. An AI combines the summaries | Only if an automatic check holds it for review, or the CEO agreed we may keep it |
| Your company's IT administrators | Possibly, under your company's usual rules for its AI platform | No | Only if the CEO shares it |

## If the CEO agrees we may keep the report

At checkout the CEO can tick an optional box (not ticked by default) letting us keep the report after delivery. We use kept reports only to improve our own work and to compare patterns across companies. We never publish a report, never name a company, and never share a report with anyone. A CEO can ask us to delete a kept report at any time.

## Service providers, and where data is processed

We use a small number of service providers to run the diagnostic:

- **Squarespace**, for our website and checkout
- **[AI provider]**, to write the report from the summaries [Mack picks at build]
- **[Hosting provider]**, for the drop-box and the status page [Mack picks at build]
- **[Email provider]**, to send the setup kit, the report and one check-in email [Mack picks at build]

Some of these providers may process information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to local laws. They may use the information only to provide their service to us. [CONFIRM each provider's data terms, and that the AI provider does not train on our data.]

## Keeping information safe

Summaries are held in a private store, reachable only by our system, and deleted on delivery. The drop-box link is private to each company. No person at Franklin Kinetics reads summaries. If information is ever lost or exposed, we will tell the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner of Canada as the law requires.

## Your choices

- **Leaders** can skip any question, stop at any time, leave anything out of their summary, edit it, or not send it at all.
- **The CEO** can ask what information we hold about their company, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete a kept report.
- Because we remove names on arrival, we can't find or return an individual leader's summary.

## Not available in Quebec

The diagnostic is currently not offered to companies based in Quebec, or to leaders based in Quebec.

## Questions or concerns

Write to **contact@franklinkinetics.com**. We aim to reply within two working days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

# Rotor Alignment Diagnostic terms

*Last updated: [date of publishing]*

These terms are between Franklin Kinetics Ltd. [CONFIRM legal name] ("we") and the company that buys the diagnostic ("you"). The person who completes checkout confirms they can agree to them for the company.

## 1. What you're buying

One Rotor Alignment Diagnostic for one company:
- A setup kit for Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot or Google Gemini
- An AI interview for each of three to eight of your leaders
- A written report, emailed to the buyer
- One restart within 12 months of purchase, if needed
- One check-in email, about two weeks after the report

## 2. Price and payment

$790 CAD, paid in full at checkout. [CONFIRM: plus GST, once the accountant confirms.]

## 3. How it works, and what you agree to do

- You install the interviewer in your company's AI platform and invite your leaders.
- You confirm you're allowed to add an assistant to that platform under your company's own rules and your agreement with that vendor.
- You send your leaders the disclosure note in the setup kit before they take part.
- You won't ask a leader to show you their summary or their interview. The diagnostic works because no one sees them.
- You invite only leaders based outside Quebec, and your company is not based in Quebec.

## 4. The three-leader minimum

The report needs at least three leaders to send their summaries. The window for summaries is 21 days from purchase. You can close it early once three are in, or extend it once by 14 days.

**If fewer than three leaders send summaries, there is no report and no refund.** Any summaries received are deleted. You can restart the diagnostic once, within 12 months of purchase, when more of your team can take part.

## 5. Your report, or your money back

If three or more leaders send their summaries and we don't deliver your report, we refund you in full.

## 6. What the report is, and isn't

The report shows where your leaders' views agree and differ, in their own words. It does not give advice, recommend a strategy, or promise any result. Decisions you make after reading it are yours.

## 7. Confidentiality and data

We handle your leaders' information as described in our privacy notice [link]. Your company is responsible for its relationship with its own employees, including telling them how the diagnostic works. We act on your behalf only to produce your report.

## 8. Who owns what

- **The report is yours.** Use it however you like inside your company.
- **The interviewer instructions and setup kit** are ours. You may use them for this diagnostic only. Please don't copy, share or resell them.

## 9. Limit of liability

Our total liability for anything to do with the diagnostic is limited to the amount you paid for it. We aren't responsible for indirect or consequential losses, such as lost profit or business decisions made after reading the report. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law doesn't allow us to limit.

## 10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms. The version in force on the day you bought applies to your purchase. These terms are governed by the laws of Saskatchewan and of Canada as they apply there.

Questions: **contact@franklinkinetics.com**
