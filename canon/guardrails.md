# Guardrails

Hard limits. Renumbered 9–15 on 2026-11-26 (there were two 8s; files dated earlier use the old numbers); 16–17 added the same day. Vera enforces them at Clearance and can veto at any stage. Only Brandon can change a guardrail, and the change must be recorded in `logs/decisions.md`.

## Identity and affiliation
1. **No implied DND/CAF endorsement or connection.** No mention of Brandon's military role, rank, unit or base in any Lab, Story, Package or marketing. Nothing that leans on it for credibility.
2. **Don't mention the human performance in military aviation course or mishap case-study facilitation** until Brandon confirms completion and decides to.

## Claims and evidence
3. **No ROI promises.** Describe what a product does, not what it will earn or save someone.
4. **No client results until they are real**, and then only with permission.
5. **No invented testimonials, reviews, customers, quotes or usage numbers.** Ever.
6. **Data claims must be traceable.** Every number in a Lab or Story cites its source in the lab folder.

## Pricing
7. **Lab products show prices. Consulting and Rotor Beta stay unpriced.** Nothing on a Lab page may imply a consulting price.
8. **A Lab never requires Brandon's time.** If a product needs his consulting time to deliver, it is consulting, not a Lab, and it stays unpriced. (A Lab's optional call CTA is not delivery; the Lab must be complete without it.)
9. **No dark patterns:** no fake scarcity, fake countdowns, pre-checked upsells, or hard-to-cancel subscriptions. Refund terms are stated plainly.

## Data and privacy
10. **Public or licensed data only.** Mack (or Ines) records the licence or terms for every source; Vera checks it independently.
11. **No profiling or targeting private individuals.** Aggregate, or stick to public entities (companies, government, public infrastructure). A map of permits is fine; a page about a person is not. Writing to a business at the address it publishes, about its business, is fine; building a file on a person is not.
12. **Customer data is minimal.** Collect only what the product needs. Never sell or share it.

## Rotor
13. **Rotor Core contents are never published** in a Lab, Story or repo file meant for the public.

## Craft
14. **Assume the reader knows more than the AI does.** Nudges, not lectures.
15. **Accessible by default:** readable contrast, alt text, works on a phone.

## Operations
16. **Every automation offer runs without daily babysitting.** It must run unattended, send its alerts to the client's own people (not Brandon), and need no daily step from Brandon. A design that needs him daily is redesigned, not sold. (Brandon, 2026-11-26.)
17. **Automation prices never appear in public.** "$1,000/month" is an internal floor, not copy. Automations are consulting and stay unpriced, like Rotor work (see 7).
