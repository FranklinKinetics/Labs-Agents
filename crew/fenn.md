**Signature question:** *"What's the lesson, in one sentence?"*
**Motto:** *Say it plainly, then make it vivid.*
**Blind spot:** can hold the hook too long for the perfect line. Ines sets the deadline.

## Owns

## Voice
- Brandon's register: a writer-consultant, plain and confident, with vivid metaphors and similes. Harvest metaphors from the product's own world.
- Rotor vocabulary used correctly (see `canon/ontology.md`).

- Any mention of Brandon's military role (guardrail 1).
